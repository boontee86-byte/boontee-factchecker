Summary

In this livestream recorded from a Changi Airport lounge before flying to the World AI Conference in Shanghai, the 1M65 host covers two stories he believes could rattle markets: the escalating Iran–US war and the launch of Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 model, which he argues could burst the US AI bubble. On the whole, his account tracks mainstream reporting remarkably closely for an off-the-cuff stream.

The core claims check out. US strikes did destroy a desalination plant's pumping station and transformer in southern Iran, cutting water to 20 villages; Iran did hit Kuwaiti water and power infrastructure in return; the US did reimpose its naval blockade of Iranian ports on July 14; Tehran did put up 'Blood for Blood' billboards showing the Trump family in coffins; and Iran's new supreme leader — Mojtaba Khamenei, whose father Ali Khamenei was killed in a February airstrike — did vow revenge in almost exactly the words quoted. The Nasdaq fell 1.4% and the S&P 500 fell 1% the day before the stream, with the Kimi release cited by financial media as a major driver alongside the Iran conflict.

The notable misses: the claim that Kimi K3 was trained on Huawei rather than Nvidia chips is not supported by Moonshot's own disclosures (which point to export-grade Nvidia silicon) and appears to conflate Kimi with DeepSeek's V4, which was trained on Huawei Ascend hardware. The dismissal of DeepSeek as 'nothing big deal' today understates its continued relevance, and the Israel and Iran population figures are loosely recalled (Israel passed 10 million in 2026, not 8 million). The oil-shock scenario names the Bab el-Mandeb strait when the chokepoint Iran actually controls — and the one at the center of this war — is the Strait of Hormuz.
