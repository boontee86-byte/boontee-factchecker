Think of CPF not as a retirement restriction but as a bond allocation. The Special Account earns 4% per year, risk-free, guaranteed by the government. These are the characteristics of a high-quality fixed-income position managed on autopilot. No rebalancing, no monitoring, no decisions needed.

## Optimisation Strategies

### Transfer OA to SA

The Ordinary Account earns 2.5%. The Special Account earns 4%. After using OA for the HDB down payment, transfer excess OA balance to SA. This is a guaranteed 1.5% annual improvement in return with zero additional risk.

The OA-to-SA transfer is irreversible. Only transfer amounts that are genuinely excess to housing needs. Once in the SA, the funds cannot be withdrawn for property or any other purpose until retirement.

### Annual SA Top-Up for Tax Relief

Voluntary top-ups to the SA qualify for income tax relief. This provides an immediate tax benefit on top of the 4% guaranteed return. Continue annual top-ups until the Full Retirement Sum (FRS) is reached, at which point further top-ups are no longer accepted.

### Spousal SA Transfer

Transfer OA balance to a spouse's SA to fund their retirement at 4% guaranteed. This is particularly useful when one partner has a higher income and the other has a lower CPF balance.

### CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS)

The 4% guaranteed SA return sets a high bar. Investing CPF funds into equities through CPFIS means giving up a risk-free 4% return for uncertain equity returns, after fees. For most people, this is hard to justify.

CPF and brokerage accounts serve different functions. CPF is the defensive foundation -- capital-protected, guaranteed, requiring no attention. The brokerage account is for concentrated growth where active decisions and conviction drive returns. Mixing these roles weakens both.

## CPF as Bond Allocation

In a traditional portfolio framework, bonds provide stability and predictable income. CPF's SA does exactly this:

- **4% guaranteed return** -- better than most bond funds after fees
- **Zero credit risk** -- backed by the Singapore government
- **Zero management effort** -- no rebalancing, no fund selection, no monitoring
- **Tax-efficient contributions** -- top-ups reduce taxable income

This frees the rest of the portfolio to take on equity risk without needing a separate bond allocation in the brokerage account.
